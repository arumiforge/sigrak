# Data pre-check: SD general subjects (CP)

Task RS-15 (D-447). Agent pre-check; people review only the flagged rows.

## 1. Header

- **Data set:** `SIGRAK-SD-2026-R1`, general SD subjects, PAI, muatan lokal and KKA.
- **Files checked:** `docs/data/curriculum/sigrak-sd-2026-r1-umum.json` (all 12 subjects, 432 topics); `docs/data/curriculum/subjects.json` (entries PP, BIN, MTK, BIG, PJOK, PAI, SB, BJW, SSD, IPAS, KKA, KTB).
- **Sources used** (page = PDF page; the 046 copy has no printed page numbers):
  - `docs/sources/Kepka_BSKAP_No_046_H_KR_2025_Capaian_Pembelajaran.pdf`, Lampiran II: PAI pp. 23–28, PP pp. 76–85, Bahasa Indonesia pp. 88–97, Matematika pp. 106–117, Bahasa Inggris pp. 126–135, IPAS pp. 146–152, Seni Musik pp. 234–241, Seni Rupa pp. 243–248, PJOK pp. 302–312, KKA pp. 338–343.
  - `docs/sources/Permendikdasmen_No_13_Tahun_2025_Perubahan_Permendikbudristek_12_2024_Kurikulum.pdf`, SD structure Tabel 1–5 and notes, pp. 15–22.
  - PAI only: Kepka BKPDM 020/2026 (`docs/sources/Salinan_Kepka_BKPDM_No._020_…pdf`) is a scan without a text layer, so its verified transcription `docs/data/curriculum/cp-pendidikan-agama-sd-bkpdm-020-2026.json` (pages 6–8) was used as a proxy.
- **Date:** 2026-11-27.
- **Method:** I extracted the decree text with `pdftotext -layout` into a scratch folder outside the repo. Python scripts (`-I`) grouped every topic's `cp_text` and `element` by subject and phase, compared element names with each decree's element list, and checked codes, phase against grade, JP sums against `allocations` and the 13/2025 tables, weekly distribution, and fokus JP. I then read each distinct `cp_text` (135 texts) against the decree's per-element CP for that phase and searched the decree for the terms that differed. TP, ATP and JP splits were checked only for internal consistency.

## 2. Summary

- **Checked:** 12 subjects; 432 topics; 135 distinct `cp_text` values; element names of 9 subjects that have a national CP (PP, BIN, MTK, BIG, PJOK, PAI, SB Musik/Rupa, IPAS, KKA); 1,372 TP and 2,021 fokus codes; JP sums for every grade and semester.
- **Flagged:** 29 rows. 4 high, 14 medium and 11 low.
- **Main finding:** no `cp_text` is the decree CP. The texts are per-grade or per-topic summaries taken from the Buku Panduan Guru (based on CP 2022–2024). The decree gives one text per element per phase. This is flag 1. The other content flags list only the places where the summary says something the decree does not, or puts it in the wrong phase.
- **Known issues** (confirmed, not counted as new):
  - **Matematika grade 4 semester 2:** now 90/90 JP in the data (K4-04…06 = 35 + 35 + 20, with a note "v5.20.545: semester 2 dikoreksi dari 80 menjadi 90 JP"). It looks closed in the data, but `PROGRESS.md` (line 68) still lists it.
  - **Tata Boga target:** confirmed and refined in flag 17.
  - **TP↔fokus links:** confirmed. None of the 2,021 `focus_items` has any field that links it to a TP.

## 4. Checked and fine

- **PP:** element names are official; phase and grade fit; grade 4–5 texts follow 046 Fase B/C apart from flags 5, 6 and 26; JP 72/72 (56 in grade 6 S2) matches 13/2025 (144/128 per year).
- **BIN:** element names are exact; phase and grade fit; JP 7/8/6 per week matches 13/2025 (252, 288, 216, 192).
- **MTK:** number ranges match 046 (Fase A to 100, Fase B to 10.000, Fase C to 1.000.000 and operations to 100.000); JP matches 13/2025 (144/180/160); grade 4 S2 is now 90.
- **BIG grades 3–6:** phase fit and 2 JP per week match 13/2025 (72/64); `subjects.json` transition follows D-410.
- **PJOK:** JP 3 per week matches (108/96); the Fase C texts for grade 5 (K5-01, -02, -04, -05) follow 3.1–3.4 closely.
- **PAI:** 5 elements per phase in the right order; phases A/B/C on grades 1–2/3–4/5–6; Akidah, Akhlak, Fikih and SPI content matches 020/2026 in substance; JP 3 per week matches (108/96).
- **SB:** element names (5 artistic elements) are correct apart from flag 20; the 54 JP semester total matches 108/96 per year (but see flag 16).
- **IPAS:** grades 3–6 only (no Fase A), as in 046 and 13/2025; 5 JP per week (180/160) matches; the combined element label fits 046's note that both elements are taught as one unit.
- **KKA:** Fase C only (grades 5–6), as in 046; grade 5 texts K5-01…04 follow 1.1–1.4 closely; topic JP (72/64) matches 2 JP per week.
- **subjects.json:** the names of the 12 subjects match 046 and 13/2025; aliases cover "Pendidikan Jasmani Olahraga dan Kesehatan".
- **Internal:** no duplicate codes; every TP and fokus code and order is sequential; phase matches grade in all 432 topics; `weekly_distribution` sums and fokus JP sums equal `jp_total` everywhere except flags 17 and 18; every semester JP sum equals its allocation where an allocation exists.
