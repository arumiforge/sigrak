- **Audience:** teachers and principals of SD (public and private).
- **Goal:** show that SD rules are built in: structure and JP, six religion subjects, guru kelas and guru mapel, regional muatan lokal, and the e-Rapor SD export.
- **Sources:** product concept (Market, Roles), D-034 (six religions), D-204 (optional English and IPAS in grades 1–2), D-218 (e-Rapor SD export), D-383 (guru kelas), `docs/final/religious-education.md`. Curriculum data covers SD/MI only (D-363, D-396).
