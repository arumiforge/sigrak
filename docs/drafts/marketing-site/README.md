## How the drafts were written

- **Claims:** only what v1 contains per `docs/final/product-concept.md`. No numbers except prices, durations and counts fixed by decisions (7-day trial, 4 wizard steps, 18 supervision instruments, 30 TKA items, 14 working days, 5 schools for Sigrak Binaan, 1-month AI usage log, 7/30-day backups, RPO 5 min / RTO 1 h). No testimonials, user counts or school names. Speed promises from the product concept's "three proofs" (for example "under a minute") are targets, not measured results, so the copy does not use them.
- **Trial length:** 7 days (D-160 overrides the 2 weeks in D-157).
- **Add-ons:** the D-158 list is only the starting configuration; the console decides (D-211). Pages tag add-on features "Fitur tambahan"; the built pages read the tags from the console mapping.
- **Terms and banned words** follow `docs/final/design-system.md`. Checked: no "siswa", "P5", "taraf serap", "absensi", "teguran", "tanda tangan elektronik", "tunggakan", "kurang bayar", "belum lunas", "cerdas", "canggih" in the Indonesian copy.
- **Legal drafts:** each carries "Versi", "Berlaku sejak: [tanggal]" and the visible note "DRAF — belum ditinjau ahli hukum". Unknown facts are placeholders; no company details were invented. Security statements describe the controls decided in D-398, D-400, D-404, D-405, D-426, D-430 and the Fondasi baseline; most are not built yet.

## Open questions for the owner

Legal review itself is not an open question here: it happens offline (D-444).

1. **Trial sign-up path.** Where does "Coba 7 hari" go (for example `app.sigrak.com/daftar`), and how does a teacher who signed up alone join their school later? The product concept says accounts come by invitation and also mentions onboarding "after sign-up"; the public CTA needs one answer.
2. **School CTA.** WhatsApp only, or also a contact form for principals and pengawas? (A form adds personal data to the privacy policy and needs Turnstile and rate limits.)
3. **"Prompt" on public pages.** The approved promise (D-017) says "tanpa menulis prompt", but the design system never shows the word "prompt". Keep the promise as written on `/` only, or reword it for the public site?
4. **BOS wording.** D-025 says sales material never presents BOS as a payment source. The drafts state "Langganan sekolah dibayar dari dana di luar BOS" on `/harga`, `/untuk-kepala-sekolah` and in the terms. Keep that explicit line, or leave BOS unmentioned?
5. **Naming Google Gemini.** The pages name "Gemini" and "Google" so teachers know which key they need. Acceptable on the public site?
6. **Panduan location.** `sigrak.com/panduan` or `app.sigrak.com/panduan` (D-389 lists `/panduan` among the app's public pages)? The menu and `/bantuan` link to it.
7. **Legal entity at launch.** D-438 plans a PT; D-395 says the owner is an individual business today. Who is named as operator and processor if the site launches before the PT exists?
8. **Visitor counter.** Fondasi §12 counts visitors with a daily salt and shows "Pengunjung hari ini / bulan ini / total" in the footer by default. Keep the counter, and show it in the footer or not? The privacy draft assumes the counter is on.
9. **Acara registration.** On-site form, WhatsApp, or an external form? This affects the privacy policy.
10. **Pilot region.** Mention Kudus as the pilot on public pages, or keep the site region-neutral?
11. **Teacher-only schools and the data agreement.** When teachers use Sigrak Guru but the school has no Sigrak Sekolah, who accepts the data-processing agreement as controller? The draft proposes acceptance by the principal in the app and leaves the interim rule as a placeholder (`perlindungan-data-murid.md` §2.2).
12. **School–parent agreement template.** Should SIGRAK give schools a template notice or agreement for parents (Fondasi F-023, needed for the school chatbot, D-394)? The DPA mentions it as optional (§4.2).
13. **Mid-year purchases, refunds, tax.** Pro-rating within a July–June year, refund terms, and whether prices include tax.
14. **Public chatbot logs.** Are visitors' typed questions stored (for improving answers), and for how long? (D-443 sends nothing to a provider; storage is still personal data if people type names or numbers.)
15. **Stories and practice pages.** Consent process for Cerita Sekolah and Praktik Baik: written permission from the school only, or also from teachers and parents; are photos of pupils ever used, or only Codex illustrations?
16. **Breach notice to schools.** How many hours after discovery does [Nama PT] promise to notify the school (placeholder in DPA §11; it must leave the school time for the 3 × 24-hour notice of UU PDP Article 46)?
